SLIDE 1: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VISION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tiketa is a Web3 event ticketing dApp engineered on Botchain that replaces
antiquated, predatory ticketing monopolies with transparent, immutable smart
contracts.

By utilizing Ethereum's ERC-1155 standard with capped supply enforcement and
atomic native settlement in BOT, Tiketa guarantees:
  * 100% counterfeit-proof digital tickets
  * Exact payment verification with zero scalper markups or hidden fees
  * True peer-to-peer asset ownership and secondary liquidity
  * Instant, automated treasury payouts directly to event organizers


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM — LEGACY TICKETING IS BROKEN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The traditional live event ticketing industry ($75B+ annual volume) is burdened
by deep structural failures:

1. RAMPANT FRAUD & COUNTERFEITING
   - Over $15B lost annually to fake tickets, duplicate QR screenshots, and scams.
   - Fans arrive at venues only to find their ticket has already been scanned.

2. PREDATORY MIDDLEMEN & JUNK FEES
   - Platforms like Ticketmaster and Eventbrite charge 15% to 35% in hidden "service fees",
     "processing fees", and "delivery fees".
   - Creators and fans both lose value to extractive intermediaries.

3. UNREGULATED BOT SCALPING
   - Sybil bot rings scoop up inventory in milliseconds and resell tickets on secondary
     markets at 300% to 1,000% markups, with 0% of profits returned to artists.

4. CUSTODIAL LOCK-IN & RESTRICTIONS
   - Users don't actually own their tickets; platforms can cancel, lock, or restrict
     ticket transfers at their sole discretion.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3: THE SOLUTION — TIKETA PROTOCOL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tiketa solves these challenges through trustless on-chain primitives:

* CRYPTOGRAPHIC CERTAINTY (ERC-1155)
  Each ticket tier (GA, VIP, Early Bird) is represented by a unique on-chain token ID
  with transparent, unchangeable supply caps enforced by bytecode logic.

* ATOMIC & EXACT SETTLEMENT
  Every purchase requires exact native token (BOT) payment calculated dynamically
  (Price x Quantity). Transactions with underpayment or overpayment are rejected.

* TRUE SOVEREIGN OWNERSHIP
  Tickets are minted directly into the attendee's self-custodial wallet (MetaMask /
  web3 wallet). Users can freely store, gift, or transfer tickets on secondary markets.

* ORGANIZER AUTONOMY
  Event creators deploy and manage their own event contracts, adjust tier prices,
  toggle sales availability, and withdraw ticket revenues without platform permission.
